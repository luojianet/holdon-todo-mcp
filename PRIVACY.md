# Privacy

HoldOn Todo MCP talks only to **HoldOn** APIs (`https://api.holdon.ltd`) using the PAT you configure in Cursor.

- The plugin does **not** send your token or todo data to Cursor Marketplace or to third parties beyond HoldOn.
- Todo content is stored in your HoldOn account under HoldOn’s product terms and privacy policy: https://www.holdon.ltd/legal/privacy.html
- Create or revoke tokens in HoldOn Notes → **Extensions & tokens**. Revoking a PAT stops this MCP immediately.
